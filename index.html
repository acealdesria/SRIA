SRIA

SRIA is a professional multi-clan community designed to bring multiple clans together in one place. Instead of having to search everywhere for a clan, SRIA gives people the ability to choose which clan or clans they want to join.

SRIA connects different clans under one multi-clan system while allowing each clan to keep its own identity. Whether you're looking for an active competitive clan, a community to chill with, or multiple clans to be part of, SRIA gives you the choice.

The clans currently available through SRIA are ROKI, TASK FORCE, SATO, CPI, AP, AHK, CHICKLAND, SRIA, IKSP, UKAP, GCC, SC and SPC.
